# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders and technical builders. Startup founders, engineers, and collaborators working in aerospace, robotics, drone tech, AI, and quantitative finance who land on the site from a shared link, a DM, or an intro. They arrive curious about who Roshan is and whether he can build the technical thing in front of them. Secondary readers (finance recruiters, peers) also pass through, but the page is tuned for the builder who wants to know "can this person ship?"

The context is a quick, self-directed visit: someone opens the link between other tabs, scrolls, and decides in seconds whether to keep reading or reach out.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Roshan A. Shah. It exists to show, fast and credibly, that he works where finance, AI, and hardware meet: PE technical work at Blue Oak, AI acquisition-sourcing tooling, aerospace and simulation software, drone/VTOL engineering, and a governance paper. Success is a founder or collaborator finishing the visit convinced he can build real systems, and reaching out. The design carries that impression; on a portfolio, the craft of the page is itself evidence.

## Brand Personality

Bold, cinematic, ambitious. The page makes a statement: large type, a live 3D background, scroll-driven motion, and an optional guided tour that walks a visitor through the work. The voice is first-person, casual, lowercase in the personal copy, concrete over label. It sounds like a builder talking about what he actually made, not an applicant summarizing himself.

Bold is not loud for its own sake. The register is cinematic-but-disciplined: motion and scale serve attention and never fight readability.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS template.** No cream/navy landing-page kit, no hero-metric blocks, no identical feature-card grids.
- **Stiff corporate resume.** Not a LinkedIn-style formal CV wall of bullet points with no personality.
- **Crypto/AI neon clichés.** No neon-on-black futurism, no gradient text, no overhyped "next-gen" framing.
- **Overdesigned / gimmicky.** Effects earn their place. No glassmorphism by default, no motion that fights reading, no decoration that outshouts the content.

The tension to hold: maximal ambition, minimal gimmick.

## Design Principles

1. **Cinematic but disciplined.** Go big on type, motion, and depth, but every effect must serve attention or comprehension. If a flourish hurts readability or reads as a gimmick, cut it.
2. **Builder to builder.** Speak like someone shipping real hardware and software. Lead with concrete mechanisms (what was built, how it works) over titles and adjectives.
3. **Show the work running.** Favor live, interactive proof (the guided tour, demos, real artifacts) over static claims. Let the page itself be a demo of craft.
4. **Honest signal.** No exaggeration, no fabricated credentials or qualifiers. Specificity is the flex; a real detail beats a superlative.
5. **Fast to the point.** A founder should grasp who Roshan is and what he builds within the first screen. Depth is available on scroll and via the tour, never required up front.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: maintain sufficient contrast for body and label text against the dark background (watch low-chroma grays on near-black). Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` across the Three.js background, Framer Motion transitions, and scroll-driven effects, since motion is heavy by default. Keep interactive controls (tour, resume, contact) keyboard-reachable and labeled. Do not rely on color alone to distinguish the amber (work focus) and cyan (personality) accents.
